The Humble Beginnings
Every great story starts with a passion, a kitchen counter, and a little bit of magic. For The Velvet Scoop, that magic began in the summer of 2018. Two childhood friends, Kabir and Meera, were disappointed by the mass-produced, artificial ice creams available in supermarkets. They missed the authentic, rich, and creamy textures of old-fashioned, churned ice cream from their grandparents' generation. Armed with an old wooden hand-cranked churn and a dream, they began experimenting in Meera’s kitchen. Their first successful batch was a simple, pure vanilla bean ice cream made with fresh cream, organic sugar, and real vanilla pods. It was a revelation.

Churning with Love and Purpose
Word quickly spread among friends and neighbors about the incredible ice cream coming out of that tiny kitchen. By 2020, what started as a weekend hobby officially transformed into a boutique neighborhood ice cream shop. We opened our very first storefront with a simple mission: to create clean, handcrafted, and honest frozen desserts without artificial flavors, dyes, or stabilizers. We believed then, as we do now, that ice cream should be made from scratch using local, wholesome ingredients that you can actually pronounce.
